🌴 Roatán, Honduras

Port Day — Mahogany Bay

Roatán is a gem — lush, green, and surrounded by one of the clearest reefs in the world. The water here is absolutely made for snorkeling, and it's calm and accessible. Wander the colorful Mahogany Bay pier area (the ship docks right there, so easy!), visit the famous iguana farm with over 3,000 wild iguanas, or find a quiet beach chair and just breathe it all in. This island has a peaceful, laid-back soul you'll love.

🤿 Snorkeling 🦎 Iguana Farm 🏖️ Beach Time 🌿 Nature Walks
5
Wed Dec 9

🌳 Belize City, Belize

Port Day — Tender Port

Belize is pure adventure and nature rolled into one. Explore ancient Mayan temples at the Altun Ha ruins — where you can actually climb a 60-foot pyramid and stand at the top. Take a peaceful river boat cruise and spot crocodiles, monkeys, manatees, and dozens of exotic birds in the wild. Or, if snorkeling calls again, the Belize Barrier Reef (the second largest in the world!) is just offshore and utterly spectacular.

🏛️ Mayan Ruins 🐊 River Wildlife Tour 🤿 Barrier Reef 🌺 Jungle Nature

Thu Dec 10

🐠 Cozumel, Mexico

Port Day

Cozumel is one of the world's top snorkeling destinations — and for good reason. The coral reefs here are alive with color and teeming with tropical fish. Explore the charming town of San Miguel on foot, visit a small local museum, or join a guided reef snorkel tour right off a beautiful white-sand beach. The island also has a beautifully peaceful, unhurried rhythm that feels made for people who appreciate quiet beauty over crowds.

🤿 World-Class Snorkeling 🐟 Coral Reef 🏛️ Local Museum 🌊 Beach Stroll

The details

Everything Taken Care Of

We've thought of everything so you don't have to. Here's how the logistics work.

🚗

Airport to Ship

When you land in Fort Lauderdale, a Lyft (or arranged car service) will get you from FLL to Port Everglades in about 10–15 minutes. It's one of the easiest cruise ports to arrive at — your luggage tags will already be on your bags, and porters take them straight to your room.

🧳

Luggage, Simplified

Pack everything you need — there's no limit to how much you bring. On embarkation day, your bags go to a porter at the pier and appear in your stateroom a few hours later. The only bag you carry yourself is your carry-on for the flight.

🛳️

Boarding the Ship

Princess makes boarding a breeze with their MedallionClass app. You'll check in online beforehand, and boarding is smooth and organized — no chaotic lines. Just show up, smile, and walk onto your floating home.

🛫

Getting Home

On the final morning, you disembark at your leisure. Your 6:45 PM flight gives you a relaxed afternoon in Fort Lauderdale before heading to the airport. No rushing, no stress — the perfect ending to a perfect week.
