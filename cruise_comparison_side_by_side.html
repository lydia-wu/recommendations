Budget reality

The math below sets the frame for both cruise options.

  • BWI to FLL flights: realistically about $600–$900 round-trip for 2 people, with Thanksgiving week likely pushing toward the high end.
  • FLL to Port Everglades: about $60–$80 total round-trip in rideshare.
  • That leaves roughly $2,200–$2,340 for the cruise portion if the full trip needs to stay under $3,100.
  • That means the cruise fare should usually stay at or below about $1,100–$1,170 per person including taxes.
  • Gratuities are extra: about $252 total for HAL or $224 total for Princess.

Ships and fit

The two-column format below keeps the ship match, onboard feel, and port personality aligned row by row.

Option A

Holland America Eurodam

The stronger lifestyle match
  • Best fit for a quieter, older-skewing, more enrichment-oriented cruise experience.
  • Mid-sized ship at about 2,100 passengers, refurbished in 2023, with a more personal feel.
  • Live music lineup feels especially well matched: Lincoln Center Stage, Billboard Onboard, and B.B. King's Blues Club.
  • EXC programming adds lectures, port context, culinary demonstrations, and documentary-style enrichment.
  • Culinary Arts Center is a notable plus for travelers who enjoy hands-on making and food experiences.
  • Key West fits the couple's likely style very well: walkable, quirky, historical, and easy to do independently.
  • Half Moon Cay offers lower-stress snorkeling in calm, clear water.
  • Main caution: Thanksgiving-week sailings can bring somewhat more families aboard than a quieter early-December departure.
Option B

Princess Star Princess

Most snorkeling, most budget room
  • Best fit if snorkeling quality and overall value matter more than ship intimacy.
  • Brand-new 2025 Sphere Class ship and the most technologically modern option here.
  • Much larger ship at 5,100+ passengers, so the tradeoff is a more resort-like atmosphere.
  • Roatan, Belize, and Cozumel are among the strongest snorkeling ports in the Caribbean.
  • Roatan stands out for reef access at West Bay Beach and relatively easy independent snorkeling.
  • Belize adds reef, wildlife, and archaeology in one place.
  • Cozumel adds very strong reef and sandbar snorkeling options with strong independent-tour value.
  • Main caution: it is a very large ship, so quiet spaces exist, but you have to seek them out intentionally.

Day-by-day comparison

Each day is aligned in matching columns so the schedules can be scanned side by side.

Option A itinerary

Day 1 — Fort Lauderdale embark

4:00 PM departure. Easier ship to learn quickly. Good first-night value in the dining room and a calm entertainment start.

Day 2 — Half Moon Cay

Private-island day with especially approachable snorkeling, clear water, and a low-stress beach setup.

Day 3 — Sea day

Strong sea-day use case: EXC lecture, cooking demo, trivia, spa time, reading, and quality live music.

Day 4 — Grand Turk

One of the easiest shore-access snorkel days, plus a small museum and an overall slower pace.

Day 5 — Amber Cove

Best used as a jumping-off point for Puerto Plata history, architecture, and culture rather than just the port area itself.

Day 6 — Thanksgiving sea day

Traditional dinner, calm onboard rhythm, and a good fit for travelers who enjoy cards, reading, and low-key activities.

Day 7 — Key West

Very walkable and independent, with strong museum, aquarium, butterfly, and browsing appeal.

Day 8 — Return to Fort Lauderdale

7:00 AM arrival and straightforward airport transfer.

Option B itinerary

Day 1 — Fort Lauderdale embark

2:00 PM departure. Bigger-ship orientation takes more effort, but the Piazza and Sanctuary set up the quiet strategy well.

Day 2 — Sea day

Quiet options exist, especially Sanctuary and Explorers Lounge, with more tech-forward ship features.

Day 3 — Sea day

Another sea day with workshops, destination talks, games, and theater — more resort energy than HAL.

Day 4 — Roatan

Excellent snorkeling day with very strong reef access and additional nature options like Gumbalimba Park.

Day 5 — Belize City

More logistics because of tendering, but a standout day for wildlife and Mayan ruins if executed well.

Day 6 — Cozumel

A signature snorkeling day with late departure, strong El Cielo appeal, and a memorable Thanksgiving port call.

Day 7 — Sea day

Final sea day with Gala-evening energy, games, shows, and optional retreat time.

Day 8 — Return to Fort Lauderdale

7:00 AM arrival and standard transfer back to FLL.

Room upgrades and transport

These details are easier to compare when the room suggestions are kept side by side.

Option A room view
  • Category E or F Large Oceanview gives a proper unobstructed window.
  • Verandah categories V, VB, VC, and VA are the better balcony targets if avoiding partially obstructed options.
  • Expected upgrade cost over interior is roughly $100–$300 per person depending on view type.
Option B room view
  • Deluxe Oceanview or Cove Balcony are the cleanest upgrade paths for an actual view.
  • Because the base fare is lower, balcony upgrades leave more room in the total budget.
  • Estimated upgrade cost is roughly $100–$200 per person.

Transport plan

Morning BWI to FLL flights are the safest fit for both sailings, with Fort Lauderdale airport to Port Everglades usually taking 10–15 minutes by rideshare. Both ships return around 7:00 AM, making a same-day afternoon flight home realistic if disembarkation runs smoothly.

Recommendation

The decision becomes simpler when framed as a personality match versus a ports-and-value match.

Why Option A wins

Option A looks like the stronger gift for the specific couple described: quieter ship, better enrichment rhythm, better cultural fit, stronger low-key onboard life, and ports that are easier to enjoy without stress.

If the point is to give them the trip they are most likely to genuinely enjoy, not just the objectively flashier itinerary, Eurodam makes the most sense.

Why Option B wins

Option B is the better answer if snorkeling quality and budget flexibility matter most. It saves substantial money while improving the reef and wildlife upside.

If the bigger ship does not bother them, Star Princess gives more port excitement per dollar.

Bottom booking note

Both options depart the Saturday before Thanksgiving, so flight pricing is a real variable. If airfare looks too high, the Dec 5 Eurodam date may preserve the preferred cruise style while lowering transport cost.
