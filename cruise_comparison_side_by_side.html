Budget reality

The math below sets the frame for both cruise options.

  • BWI to FLL flights: realistically about $600–$900 round-trip for 2 people, with Thanksgiving week likely pushing toward the high end.
  • FLL to Port Everglades: about $60–$80 total round-trip in rideshare.
  • That leaves roughly $2,200–$2,340 for the cruise portion if the full trip needs to stay under $3,100.
  • That means the cruise fare should usually stay at or below about $1,100–$1,170 per person including taxes.
  • Gratuities are extra: about $252 total for HAL or $224 total for Princess.

Ships and fit

The two-column format below keeps the ship match, onboard feel, and port personality aligned row by row.

Option A

Holland America Eurodam

The stronger lifestyle match
  • Best fit for a quieter, older-skewing, more enrichment-oriented cruise experience.
  • Mid-sized ship at about 2,100 passengers, refurbished in 2023, with a more personal feel.
  • Live music lineup feels especially well matched: Lincoln Center Stage, Billboard Onboard, and B.B. King's Blues Club.
  • EXC programming adds lectures, port context, culinary demonstrations, and documentary-style enrichment.
  • Culinary Arts Center is a notable plus for travelers who enjoy hands-on making and food experiences.
  • Key West fits the couple's likely style very well: walkable, quirky, historical, and easy to do independently.
  • Half Moon Cay offers lower-stress snorkeling in calm, clear water.
  • Main caution: Thanksgiving-week sailings can bring somewhat more families aboard than a quieter early-December departure.
Option B

Princess Star Princess

Most snorkeling, most budget room
  • Best fit if snorkeling quality and overall value matter more than ship intimacy.
  • Brand-new 2025 Sphere Class ship and the most technologically modern option here.
  • Much larger ship at 5,100+ passengers, so the tradeoff is a more resort-like atmosphere.
  • Roatan, Belize, and Cozumel are among the strongest snorkeling ports in the Caribbean.
  • Roatan stands out for reef access at West Bay Beach and relatively easy independent snorkeling.
  • Belize adds reef, wildlife, and archaeology in one place.
  • Cozumel adds very strong reef and sandbar snorkeling options with strong independent-tour value.
  • Main caution: it is a very large ship, so quiet spaces exist, but you have to seek them out intentionally.

Day-by-day comparison

These are the restored full itinerary details, kept in the same side-by-side format so you can compare each day without losing the original nuance.

Option A itinerary

Day 1 — Sat, Nov 21: Fort Lauderdale (embark)

Departure at 4:00 PM. Arrive by noon to check in, explore the ship, and attend the mandatory safety drill. The Eurodam is easy to navigate. Have dinner in the Dining Room that night — five-course menus designed by HAL's Culinary Council chefs, and no extra charge. Evening: World Stage entertainment or a live Lincoln Center Stage performance.

Day 2 — Sun, Nov 22: Half Moon Cay (RelaxAway), Bahamas — 9 AM to 5 PM

HAL's private island. The snorkeling here is genuinely wonderful — the Bonefish Lagoon is teeming with marine life. Guests can travel by boat to a site off the coast of Half Moon Cay to snorkel in crystal-clear waters with colored fish, anemones, and coral. Professional snorkeling guides assist guests, and friendly stingrays are common sightings. The HAL snorkel tour runs about $65–75/person and must be booked through HAL. They can also rent kayaks, lounge on white-sand beach, or take the stingray encounter excursion. A couple who loved snorkeling will probably rate this as the highlight of the trip. Note: November weather in the Bahamas is warm and generally calm, though brief showers are possible.

Day 3 — Mon, Nov 23: Day at Sea

Full day at sea. This is where HAL really earns its reputation. Options for this day: an EXC lecture about the upcoming port, a hands-on cooking demonstration at the Culinary Arts Center, trivia tournaments, or simply reading in the Crow's Nest observation lounge at the ship's bow. The Greenhouse Spa has thermal pools open daily. In the evening, live music at B.B. King's Blues Club or the Lincoln Center Stage string quartet.

Day 4 — Tue, Nov 24: Grand Turk, Turks & Caicos — 8 AM to 5 PM

Grand Turk is a small, unhurried island with one of the best shore-accessible snorkel reefs in the Caribbean — literally steps from the pier. The reef is only 200 yards from shore and drops off dramatically; reef snorkeling tours run $55–75/person through HAL or independent operators. The island also has a small but excellent Turks & Caicos National Museum, housed in a 19th-century colonial building, with exhibits on the island's salt industry history and a re-creation of a Spanish galleon wreck discovered offshore. Light hike, fresh air, manageable pace — ideal for this couple.

Day 5 — Wed, Nov 25: Amber Cove, Dominican Republic — 8 AM to 5 PM

Amber Cove is a purpose-built cruise port near Puerto Plata. The port itself has a pleasant pool complex, a good farmers' market, and local craft vendors. The real draw for this couple is the excursion into Puerto Plata, which has a beautiful 16th-century Spanish fort, a charming Victorian-era town center with gingerbread architecture, and La Isabela, the first European settlement in the Americas, with a small museum. A half-day cultural/history excursion to Puerto Plata runs $60–85/person through HAL.

Day 6 — Thu, Nov 26: Thanksgiving, Day at Sea

HAL will likely do a traditional Thanksgiving dinner in the main Dining Room — a lovely touch for a retirement gift trip. The kitchen puts on an impressive feast. Full day for spa, reading, trivia, and games. HAL's card room and board game library are well-stocked.

Day 7 — Fri, Nov 27: Key West, Florida — 8 AM to 6 PM

Key West is a perfect port for this couple. It's compact, flat, walkable, and full of character. No car needed — rent bikes for easy exploration, or just walk. Must-see: the Hemingway Home and Museum, the Key West Aquarium, and Mallory Square for the sunset celebration. The Key West Butterfly and Nature Conservatory is extraordinary — a climate-controlled dome with hundreds of free-flying tropical butterflies and birds, the kind of quiet, beautiful, hands-on natural experience this couple will love. No ship excursion needed; all of this is independent and walkable.

Day 8 — Sat, Nov 28: Return to Fort Lauderdale

Arrive 7:00 AM. Disembark by mid-morning. Uber to FLL, then flight home.

Option B itinerary

Day 1 — Sat, Nov 21: Fort Lauderdale (embark)

Departure 2:00 PM. The Star Princess is massive — plan to explore and orient yourself in the afternoon. The Piazza is immediately impressive and a good quiet gathering spot. Sanctuary adults-only retreat ($30–40/day) is worth considering for a quieter experience. Muster drill is digital via the Princess app now, making embarkation much less crowded. Dinner in the Dining Room.

Day 2 — Sun, Nov 22: Day at Sea

A full sea day crossing south. The Sanctuary adults-only sun deck is lovely and quiet. Princess's Explorers Lounge is well-stocked for reading. The Princess MedallionClass technology is interesting — wearable tracker that delivers room service anywhere on the ship and unlocks the door. The Piazza has excellent live music in the evenings. The ship offers trivia, art classes, and enrichment lectures on the ports ahead.

Day 3 — Mon, Nov 23: Day at Sea

Second sea day. Princess's enrichment programming includes culinary demonstrations, photography workshops, and destination talks. If they enjoy strategy games, the Game Room typically has extensive board game libraries for borrowing — exactly the kind of quiet activity they'd love. Evening production show in the theater.

Day 4 — Tue, Nov 24: Roatan, Honduras — 8 AM to 5 PM

West Bay Beach — accessible by local taxi or excursion van from the pier — is one of the most beautiful snorkeling beaches in the Caribbean. The reef begins about 30 yards from shore and the water is perfectly clear. Independent snorkeling tours cost about $50/person compared to $89–99 through the ship and are generally reliable. Butterfly Garden at Gumbalimba Park is also excellent for the couple's love of animals and nature. The Fort at Roatan Hill gives a gentle view of the island's landscape. Easy, beautiful, not exhausting.

Day 5 — Wed, Nov 25: Belize City, Belize — 7 AM to 6 PM

The ship tenders into Belize, which means taking a small tender boat to shore — manageable, but it does require patience. This is best explored via ship or trusted independent excursion because there's no time to waste. Top option: Altun Ha Mayan Ruins and Belize River Wildlife Safari — a half-day excursion that visits one of the most accessible and photogenic Mayan sites, then a boat safari down the Belize River for howler monkey sightings, crocodiles, birds, and manatees. This combines archaeology, animals, and light-effort nature in one excursion.

Day 6 — Thu, Nov 26: Cozumel, Mexico — 9 AM to 7 PM (late stay)

The late-night departure is unusual and wonderful — it means the Cozumel pier at night with its lit-up carnival atmosphere. The El Cielo snorkeling tour from independent operators in Cozumel runs about $50/person versus $89–99 through the ship. El Cielo is famous for its shallow, starfish-carpeted sandbar and then reef snorkeling by boat — perfect for someone re-entering snorkeling after a long break. The San Gervasio Mayan ruins are also excellent and very affordable to visit independently. Cozumel's downtown has excellent local craft markets for the hands-on shoppers.

Day 7 — Fri, Nov 27: Day at Sea

Final sea day. Princess traditionally does a Gala evening — their version of a formal dinner night. Even if they do not care about dressing up, the food is excellent. Game room, puzzle lounge, production show, or just the Sanctuary all work well here.

Day 8 — Sat, Nov 28: Fort Lauderdale

Arrive 7:00 AM. Disembark by mid-morning.

Practical read

Yes — November absolutely still allows snorkeling and outdoor activities on these routes. The more realistic caveat is not temperature but weather variability: wind, brief showers, and occasional rougher water can affect a specific excursion day, especially in shoulder-season Caribbean weather. Water temperature itself is not the limiting factor here.

Transport plan

Morning BWI to FLL flights are the safest fit for both sailings, with Fort Lauderdale airport to Port Everglades usually taking 10–15 minutes by rideshare. Both ships return around 7:00 AM, making a same-day afternoon flight home realistic if disembarkation runs smoothly.

Recommendation

The decision becomes simpler when framed as a personality match versus a ports-and-value match.

Why Option A wins

Option A looks like the stronger gift for the specific couple described: quieter ship, better enrichment rhythm, better cultural fit, stronger low-key onboard life, and ports that are easier to enjoy without stress.

If the point is to give them the trip they are most likely to genuinely enjoy, not just the objectively flashier itinerary, Eurodam makes the most sense.

Why Option B wins

Option B is the better answer if snorkeling quality and budget flexibility matter most. It saves substantial money while improving the reef and wildlife upside.

If the bigger ship does not bother them, Star Princess gives more port excitement per dollar.

Bottom booking note

Both options depart the Saturday before Thanksgiving, so flight pricing is a real variable. If airfare looks too high, the Dec 5 Eurodam date may preserve the preferred cruise style while lowering transport cost.
