⚠️ No shade on top — sunscreen up before you leave the car. Rattlesnake habitat, stay on trail.

10:00–11:15a
Clear Creek Whitewater Park
Tubing + Creek Time

Before you go: Check flag status at VisitGolden.com — green flag = open. No reservation needed.

You + babies: Walk the paved path on the north bank of the creek — flat, stroller-friendly, gorgeous. Watch the tubers from the bank and let the babies splash in the shallows. There's a big grassy park, sand volleyball, and a baseball diamond right there.

🛟 Spouse's Rental — Where to Go:
Option 1 — Golden River Sports (recommended): 806 Washington Ave, Golden (2 blocks north of the creek, between 8th & 9th St). Walk in, sign waiver, rent tube + life jacket + helmet. No reservations for groups under 15. Open Sat 10am–6pm. Call: (303) 215-9386.

Option 2 — Adventure West: Located at the rear of 423 10th St, right next to Vanover Park. Tube + 2 shuttle rides = ~$39/person on weekends. Book online to skip the line.
💡 Tip: Shoes required — no flip flops for tubers. Spouse should leave valuables in the car or rent a locker ($5) at GRS. The float takes about 1 hour end to end, then walk back or use the shuttle.

Lunch in Evergreen
Choose Your Spot

🥇 First Choice: Marshdale Burger Co.
Locally sourced, pasture-raised, grass-fed Colorado ranch beef. Everything scratch-made. Family-friendly with outdoor patio. $10–20/person. Call ahead: (303) 674-1314

🅿️ Parking — Marshdale: Located on Hwy 73 in Marshdale (on the way to Evergreen Lake). Small parking lot directly in front of the restaurant off Hwy 73. If full, pull off onto the shoulder — this is a casual mountain roadside spot.
🍽️ What to Order: The rotating monthly burger + tater tots + smoked mac & cheese. The green chili is award-winning — add it to your burger. Brioche bun, grass-fed beef. Skip the fryer-heavy sides if you're avoiding seed oils — go for the side salad or coleslaw instead.
🔀 Alternate: Murphy's Mountain Grill
27906 CO-74, Evergreen · (720) 524-8621 · Open Sat 11am–9pm
More elevated, creek-side patio on Bear Creek, rustic mountain vibe. Slightly more upscale than Marshdale.

What to Order: The Elk Black & Bleu burger (Colorado farm-raised elk, garlic blue cheese aioli — reviewers call it the best burger in Colorado), sweet potato tots, and the BBQ half chicken with finger potatoes. The taco wontons are a beloved starter. Note: slightly pricier and can have a wait — arrive right at 12pm.
🅿️ Parking — Murphy's: Street parking on CO-74 in downtown Evergreen — can be competitive. Best bet: arrive early and park along the main strip. The restaurant is small so a short wait is possible on weekends.

1:30–2:45p
Evergreen Lake
Canoe + Walk

Rent a canoe or kayak right at the Lake House — no reservation needed, first-come first-served. Life jackets included in rental price. Paddle up Bear Creek inlet for a gorgeous, calm stretch. Walk the 1.25-mile trail around the lake after. Watch for elk — they actually bathe in this lake. 🦌

⚠️ No swimming — this is the community's drinking water source. Wristbands required for all boaters.

🅿️ Parking: Navigate to 29612 Upper Bear Creek Rd, Evergreen, CO 80439 (Evergreen Lake House). The parking lot fills fast on weekends — if it's full, EPRD closes it and directs you to the FREE shuttle from Stagecoach Park at 3229 El Pinal Dr, Evergreen (runs 10am–5pm). Budget 10 extra minutes if you have to shuttle. Arriving around 1:30pm on a Saturday should be okay.
🛶 Rental — Where to Go: Walk directly to the Lake House / Boat House at the water's edge — you'll see it right from the parking lot. Sign the online waiver in advance to save time: smartwaiver link. Rentals start at $25. Call the Boating Hotline before you go: (720) 880-1391.

Golden Saigon Takeout
Dinner · Aurora

Family-owned Vietnamese gem in Aurora, open since 1995. 4.8★ on Google. Order ahead by phone — they do takeout beautifully. Address: 2648 S Parker Rd Unit 2, Aurora, CO 80014.

Eat on the deck with the cobbler waiting in the oven. Perfect end to the day. 🌅

🍽️ What to Order (reviewer favorites):
• Chicken with Lemongrass Butter — the most-mentioned dish, consistently called incredible
• Pho Tai (beef pho with rare steak) — husband's favorite per dozens of reviews
• Fried Dumplings — complimentary ones come with your meal, but order extra
• Seafood Curry — unique to this restaurant, a cult favorite
• Shrimp Spring Rolls — fresh and light
• Homemade Ginger Ale (house-made, non-alcoholic) — do NOT skip this, reviewers are obsessed
• Crab Rangoon — another crowd pleaser
🅿️ Parking (if picking up): Strip mall parking lot at 2648 S Parker Rd — free, easy, plenty of spaces. Pull up to Unit 2.

Cobbler + Movie Night
Wind down

Kids to bed. Then: warm blueberry sourdough cobbler with vanilla ice cream, a movie, and every inch of the couch. You've earned it.

🫐 Friday Night Prep: Mix sourdough discard + flour + buttermilk + honey in a bowl. Cover, leave on counter 8–12 hrs. Saturday: add baking powder, salt, cold butter → lay over blueberry filling in pan → refrigerate. Bake at 350°F for ~30 min Saturday evening. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
