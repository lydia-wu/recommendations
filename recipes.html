Baked Butternut with Spiced Pears
GF · No added sugar · No cornstarch · Serves 1 + extra pears · 385 cal · 31g protein
+

Start your day with steadier blood sugar, calmer energy, and a grounded nervous system. In Traditional Chinese medicine, pears are a classic autumn food. Stewed, they're gentle on digestion and moistening, and paired with warming spices they make this bowl nourishing and supportive of digestion.

Macros (per serving)
  • 385 calories · 31g protein · 32g carbs · 12g fat · 10g fibre
Baked Butternut
  • ¾ cup roasted butternut squash purée (canned works too)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp oat flour (certified gluten-free; just blended oats)
  • ½ tsp vanilla · ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of nutmeg · pinch of salt
Stewed Cardamom Pears (makes 4 servings)
  • 2 medium pears, diced
  • ½ tsp cardamom
  • 1 tsp freshly grated ginger
  • Pinch of salt · 2 tbsp water
Topping
  • ⅓ cup skyr or Greek yogurt mixed with ½ scoop vanilla protein powder (whey)
  • 1 tbsp chopped pecans
Instructions
  1. Halve the squash, scoop out the seeds, and roast cut side down at 400°F for 40–50 minutes until very soft. Blend until smooth, or use canned.
  2. Whisk the purée, egg, oat flour, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Pour into a small oven-safe bowl and bake at 350°F for 28–30 minutes until set. A very slight wobble in the center is okay. Rest 5 minutes.
  3. While it bakes, add pears, cardamom, ginger, salt, and water to a small pot. Cover and stew on low about 15 minutes until soft.
  4. Top the baked butternut with the yogurt protein topping, a quarter of the stewed pears, and the pecans.
Notes
  • Eat right away, or cover and refrigerate overnight and reheat in the morning

Southern Chicken and Dumplings
Serves 6 · Creamy broth · Fluffy drop dumplings
+
Chicken & Broth
  • 3 lbs bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 bay leaves · 1 tsp dried thyme · 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1½ tsp salt (plus more to taste)
Stew Base
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 celery ribs, diced · 3 carrots, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
Dumplings
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder · ½ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp cold butter, cubed
  • ¾ cup cold whole milk
Instructions
  1. Poach (25 min): Add chicken, broth, bay leaves, thyme, pepper, and salt to a large Dutch oven. Bring to a boil, reduce to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook until chicken is cooked through and tender.
  2. Shred (10 min): Move chicken to a plate. Strain the broth into a bowl and set aside. When cool enough to handle, discard skin and bones and shred the meat.
  3. Sauté (8 min): In the empty pot over medium heat, melt butter. Cook onion, celery, and carrots until softened. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
  4. Base (10 min): Sprinkle in ¼ cup flour and stir constantly 1–2 minutes. Slowly whisk in the strained broth until smooth. Simmer until slightly thickened.
  5. Finish (2 min): Stir in shredded chicken and cream. Taste and add more salt and pepper if needed. Keep at a low simmer.
  6. Dumpling dough: Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt. Cut in cold butter with a fork until crumbly. Stir in cold milk just until a soft, sticky dough forms. Do not overmix.
  7. Cook dumplings (15 min): Drop heaping spoonfuls on top of the simmering stew. Cover tightly and do NOT lift the lid until puffed and cooked through.
  8. Serve: Sprinkle with parsley and lots of black pepper. Serve hot.
Tips
  • Keep the stew at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil, so dumplings steam instead of falling apart
  • Cold butter and cold milk give the fluffiest dumplings
  • Don't overwork the dough. Halve the dumpling quantity for more manageable dough
  • Leftovers thicken overnight. Thin with a splash of broth when reheating
  • Shortcut: rotisserie chicken (~4 cups shredded) plus store-bought broth cuts the time nearly in half

Whole Grain Banana Bread with Hemp Hearts
100% whole wheat · Seed-oil-free · Uses discard · 1 large + 1 small rectangle snapware
+
Ingredients
  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed (~340g)
  • 2.82 oz maple syrup
  • 2.12 oz coconut oil or butter, melted
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3.53 oz sourdough discard
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 6.35 oz whole wheat flour
  • 1.41 oz hemp hearts (plus extra for topping)
  • 1 tsp baking soda · 1 tsp cinnamon · ½ tsp fine salt
Instructions
  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5 loaf pan.
  2. Whisk mashed bananas, maple syrup, melted oil or butter, eggs, discard, and vanilla.
  3. Stir in flour, hemp hearts, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt until just combined. Don't overmix.
  4. Pour into pan, sprinkle extra hemp hearts on top, and bake ~1 hour until a toothpick comes out clean. Cover loosely with foil if the top browns too fast.
  5. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then on a rack before slicing.
Notes
  • Whole wheat soaks up moisture, so the loaf is best on day 2
  • Freezes well sliced

Roasted Potato Chicken Leek Soup
One pot · Uses leftover roasted potatoes · Serves ~4
+
Ingredients
  • 1¼ tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 2 large leeks, white & light green parts, sliced and rinsed well (~300g)
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 15 oz roasted potatoes
  • 3½ cups chicken broth
  • 15 oz boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs
  • ½ tsp dried thyme · ½ tsp salt · ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ⅝ cup heavy cream or whole milk
Instructions
  1. Melt butter/oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook leeks until soft, ~6 minutes. Add garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add broth, chicken, thyme, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil, then simmer until chicken is cooked through (165°F).
  3. Remove chicken, shred with two forks, and set aside.
  4. Add roasted potatoes and simmer so they soak up the flavor.
  5. Mash some potatoes right in the pot for a creamier texture.
  6. Return chicken to pot, stir in cream or milk, and warm 5 minutes. Taste and adjust salt.
Notes
  • Roasted potatoes are often seasoned — taste before adding extra salt
  • Blend smooth for a toddler-friendly version

Classic Crème Brûlée
Serves 6 · 300°F · Chill 4+ hrs · Torch right before serving
+
Ingredients
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 5 egg yolks
  • ½ cup granulated sugar (custard)
  • 1 vanilla bean, split & scraped (or 1 tbsp vanilla extract)
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 6 tbsp granulated sugar (topping, 1 tbsp per ramekin)
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C). Place 6 ramekins in a deep baking dish. Boil a kettle of water.
  2. Heat cream with vanilla and salt until steaming, not boiling. Remove from heat and steep 10 minutes if using a bean.
  3. Whisk egg yolks with sugar until smooth and pale. Slowly stream in warm cream while whisking. Strain through a sieve.
  4. Divide among ramekins. Pour hot water into the baking dish to come halfway up the sides. Bake until set at the edges with a slight jiggle in the center.
  5. Cool on a rack, then refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
  6. Pat tops dry. Sprinkle each with 1 tbsp sugar in an even layer. Torch until deeply golden and bubbling, or broil 2–3 minutes. Rest 5 minutes until crust hardens, then serve.
Notes
  • Make custard up to 2 days ahead — only torch right before serving
  • No torch? Broil on the top rack with ramekins on a tray of ice
  • Egg whites freeze well for another recipe

Fermented Ginger Hemp Pumpkin Bread
4-hr ferment · Sourdough · Ginger glaze · 350°F · 55–65 min
+
Ferment (4 hrs)
  • 4¾ oz whole wheat flour
  • 4¾ oz bread flour
  • 6⅓ oz active sourdough starter (fed 4–6 hrs, bubbly)
  • 8⅝ oz pumpkin puree (not pie filling)
  • 5⅝ oz maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp fresh grated ginger
Add Before Baking
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2.8 oz melted butter or coconut oil
  • 1½ tsp ground ginger · 1½ tsp cinnamon · ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp baking soda · ½ tsp baking powder · ¾ tsp fine sea salt
  • 1¾ oz hemp hearts (+ extra for topping)
  • 1⅜ oz chopped crystallized ginger (optional)
Ginger Glaze
  • 2⅛ oz powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • ½ tsp fresh ginger juice (or ¼ tsp ground ginger)
  • Pinch of salt
Instructions
  1. Stir flours, starter, pumpkin, maple syrup, and fresh ginger until no dry flour remains. Cover and ferment 4 hours at ~70°F until slightly puffy with a few bubbles. (6 hours for tangier.)
  2. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5 loaf pan and line with parchment.
  3. Stir eggs, melted butter/oil, spices, baking soda, baking powder, and salt into the fermented batter. Fold in hemp hearts and crystallized ginger. Mix just until combined.
  4. Pour into pan, sprinkle extra hemp hearts on top. Bake 55–65 min until a toothpick comes out clean. Tent with foil if top browns too fast.
  5. Cool in pan 15 min, then on a rack until completely cool — a warm loaf will melt the glaze right off.
  6. Whisk glaze ingredients until smooth and pourable. Drizzle over cooled loaf. Sets in ~15 minutes. Sprinkle crystallized ginger on wet glaze for extra flair.
Notes
  • Keeps 3 days on the counter
  • Freeze unglazed slices — glaze after thawing

Focaccia B (Sourdough): Stone-Ground Whole Wheat Blend
Active starter, no yeast · Nuttier & tangier · 1 large + 1 square snapware · 450°F · 20–25 min
+
Ingredients
  • 290g (~10 oz) bread flour
  • 149g (5¼ oz) stone-ground whole wheat flour
  • 345g (scant 1½ cups) warm water
  • 100g (3.5 oz, ~½ cup) active sourdough starter (fed 4–8 hrs ago, bubbly and doubled)
  • 2 tsp fine salt
  • 6 tbsp olive oil, divided: 2 tbsp in dough · 2 tbsp in pan · 2 tbsp on top
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced (stirred into top oil)
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tsp flaky salt for topping
Instructions
  1. Stir together both flours, salt, starter, warm water, and 2 tbsp olive oil until no dry flour remains. It will be sticky and shaggy.
  2. Cover and rest so the coarse bran can soak up water. Check texture — should be loose and sticky, not stiff. If dry, add water 1 tbsp at a time.
  3. Bulk ferment at room temp (~75°F) 4–6 hours until doubled and bubbly. Or refrigerate overnight for a tangier dough, then let it sit out 1 hour before panning. Go by how the dough looks, not the clock — whole wheat ferments faster.
  4. Spread 2 tbsp oil in a 9x13 pan, coating the sides.
  5. Scrape dough into pan. Fold a few times, flip smooth side up, gently stretch toward corners. If it springs back, rest and try again.
  6. Cover and let puff 1–2 hours until jiggly and ~1.5x taller. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C) near the end.
  7. Stir minced garlic into remaining 2 tbsp oil. Oil your fingers, press deep dimples all over, then spoon garlic oil over the top. Sprinkle with thyme and flaky salt.
  8. Bake on the middle rack 20–25 minutes until deep golden brown (start checking at 20). Internal temp should reach 205–210°F. Tent loosely with foil for the last 10 minutes if the top browns too fast.
  9. Lift onto a rack right away so the bottom stays crisp. Cool a few minutes, then slice.
Notes
  • Adapted from Focaccia B: yeast removed, flour and water trimmed to account for the starter
  • If sluggish after 6 hours, give it more time — a cool kitchen slows sourdough a lot

Focaccia B: Stone-Ground Whole Wheat Blend
Test batch · Nuttier & heartier · 1 large + 1 square snapware · 450°F · 20–25 min
+
Ingredients
  • 12 oz bread flour
  • 5¼ oz stone-ground whole wheat flour
  • 1⅔ cup warm water
  • 1½ tsp instant yeast
  • 2 tsp fine salt
  • 6 tbsp olive oil, divided: 2 tbsp in dough · 2 tbsp in pan · 2 tbsp on top
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced (stirred into top oil)
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tsp flaky salt for topping
Instructions
  1. Stir together both flours, yeast, and fine salt. Add warm water and 2 tbsp olive oil. Stir until no dry flour remains — dough will be sticky and shaggy.
  2. Cover and let sit so the coarse bran soaks up water. Check texture — should be loose and sticky, not stiff. If dry, add water 1 tbsp at a time.
  3. Cover and ferment at room temp until doubled and bubbly. Stone-ground flour can ferment faster — start checking early.
  4. Spread 2 tbsp oil in a 9x13 pan, coating sides too.
  5. Scrape dough into pan. Fold a few times, flip smooth side up, gently stretch toward corners. If it springs back, rest and try again.
  6. Cover and let puff until jiggly and ~1.5x taller. Whole wheat may need longer. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C) near the end.
  7. Stir minced garlic into remaining 2 tbsp oil. Oil your fingers and press deep dimples all over. Spoon garlic oil over top, sprinkle with thyme and flaky salt.
  8. Bake on the middle rack 20–25 minutes until deep golden brown (start checking at 20). Internal temp should reach 205–210°F. Tent loosely with foil for the last 10 minutes if the top browns too fast.
  9. Lift onto a rack right away so the bottom stays crisp. Cool a few minutes, then slice.
Test Batch Notes
  • Bake side by side with Batch A — same oven, pan size, and toppings so flour is the only variable
  • Compare rise time, crumb, crust, and flavor between the two
  • If B turns out great, try 250g whole wheat next time with ~440ml water

Easy Butter Chicken
One pan · Weeknight friendly · ~1 pack Costco chicken thighs
+
Ingredients
  • 24 oz boneless chicken thighs, cut in bite-size pieces
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tsp garam masala · ½ tsp turmeric · 1 tsp chili powder (optional)
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced · 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 14 oz crushed tomatoes
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar
Instructions
  1. Toss chicken with yogurt, garam masala, turmeric, chili powder, and salt. Let sit while you prep — or overnight if you have time.
  2. Melt half the butter in a large pan over medium-high. Brown the chicken ~5 minutes. Set aside.
  3. In the same pan, melt remaining butter. Cook onion until soft, then add garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes and honey. Simmer until thickened. Add chicken back in and cook through.
  5. Stir in heavy cream and warm 2 minutes. Taste, adjust salt. Serve over rice with naan.
Notes
  • Shortcut: skip the marinade, just toss chicken in spices and salt right before cooking
  • Freezes well up to 3 months

4-Hour Sourdough Pancakes
Vegan · Coconut milk · Whole wheat · Strict fast compliant
+
Ingredients
  • 2¼ cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup active sourdough starter
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) unsweetened coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil, melted and cooled
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • ¾ tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp water, if needed to thin
Instructions
  1. Whisk together flour, starter, coconut milk, coconut oil, and maple syrup until smooth. Cover loosely and let sit at room temp for 4 hours until slightly bubbly and tangy-smelling.
  2. Right before cooking, gently fold in salt and baking soda — batter will bubble up. Add water 1 tbsp at a time if too thick.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat. Pour ¼ cup batter per pancake.
  4. Cook until bubbles form on top and edges set, then flip.
  5. Cook second side until golden brown.
Notes
  • Shake the coconut milk can well before opening — it separates
  • Oil is cut back to 1 tbsp because coconut milk is much richer than oat/soy milk
  • If batter thickens after fermenting, add water 1 tbsp at a time

No-Rise Sourdough Discard Pizza Dough
Bread flour · Mix, roll, bake · Under 30 min · Makes 2
+
Ingredients
  • 11 oz bread flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder · 1 tsp salt
  • 7.1 oz plain yogurt or sourdough discard
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ¼ cup water, add as needed
Instructions
  1. Whisk bread flour, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl.
  2. Add yogurt or discard and olive oil, then mix. Add water gradually until a soft dough forms.
  3. Knead briefly, about 1 minute, just until smooth.
  4. Divide into 2 balls and roll each out thin on a floured surface.
  5. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C). Top with sauce and toppings, bake on a hot sheet or stone until golden.
Notes
  • Bread flour feels slightly tighter than all-purpose — a splash more water keeps it easy to roll
  • Discard doesn't need to be active or bubbly — it just adds tang

Gourmet Sourdough Pineapple Upside-Down Cake
50/50 whole wheat · Overnight ferment · Frozen cherries · 350°F
+
Overnight Batter Base
  • 5¼ oz active sourdough starter (100% hydration)
  • 17 oz plain whole milk kefir (1 full pint)
  • 3½ oz King Arthur bread flour
  • 3½ oz freshly ground whole wheat flour
Morning Add-Ins
  • 4⅝ oz granulated sugar
  • 3⅛ oz coconut oil, melted and cooled
  • 2 large eggs · 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp baking soda · ½ tsp fine sea salt · ¼ tsp cardamom
Caramel Topping
  • 1.9 oz coconut oil, solid
  • 3½ oz brown sugar, packed
  • 8 pineapple rings, drained very well
  • 12 frozen dark sweet cherries, thawed and patted very dry
Instructions
  1. Night before: Whisk starter, kefir, bread flour, and whole wheat flour until smooth. Cover and ferment at room temp 8–12 hours. (12 is ideal — don't exceed 14 in a warm kitchen.)
  2. Morning: Preheat oven to 350°F. Whisk sugar, coconut oil, eggs, and vanilla into the fermented batter. Sprinkle in baking soda, salt, and cardamom — whisk just until incorporated. Don't overmix once the baking soda goes in.
  3. In a 9-inch oven-safe skillet, melt coconut oil over medium heat. Add brown sugar and stir until dissolved and bubbling, ~2 min. Remove from heat and cool 3 minutes.
  4. Lay pineapple rings over the caramel. Press cherries into the holes and gaps. Cherries must be very dry or the caramel will weep.
  5. Pour batter over fruit and smooth the top. Bake 40–48 min until center is set and toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Cool in pan exactly 10 minutes. Run a knife around the edges, place a plate firmly on top, and flip in one confident motion. Lift the pan slowly.
Notes
  • 🍒 Cherries: Squeeze thawed cherries in a paper towel until almost no juice comes out — #1 key to a clean topping
  • 🌾 Starter: Recently-fed and bubbly is best. Discard works too but gives milder tang
  • 🥥 Coconut oil: Refined = neutral flavor. Unrefined adds a subtle coconut note that's lovely with pineapple
  • ✨ Cardamom: The gourmet touch — echoes the pineapple without overpowering. Don't skip it!

Egg-Free Chocolate Chip Cookies (Kefir)
Bread flour · Chewy & tender · 375°F
+
Ingredients
  • 185g King Arthur bread flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda · ½ tsp salt
  • 80g coconut oil, melted
  • 100g brown sugar · 50g white sugar
  • 150ml kefir
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 175g chocolate chips
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Whisk bread flour, baking soda, and salt together in a bowl.
  3. Stir together coconut oil, both sugars, kefir, and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Pour wet into dry and stir until just combined. Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Cover dough and refrigerate — thickens the dough and deepens flavor. Longer = thicker cookies.
  6. Drop rounded tablespoons 2 inches apart. Bake until edges are golden but centers look just barely set.
  7. Cool on the pan before transferring — they firm up as they cool.
Notes
  • Brown sugar is key for chewiness — don't swap it all for white
  • Slightly underbake — centers will look underdone when you pull them. That's right!
  • For thicker cookies, chill the dough longer than 30 min

Sourdough Slider Buns
Bread flour · Yogurt & coconut oil · Makes 12
+
Ingredients
  • 100g active sourdough starter (fed 4–8 hrs before)
  • 200ml warm water
  • 60ml plain whole milk yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 45g coconut oil, melted and cooled
  • 1 large egg
  • 420g bread flour
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 1 egg + 1 tbsp water (egg wash)
  • Sesame seeds or flaky salt (optional topping)
Instructions
  1. Whisk together starter, water, yogurt, honey, coconut oil, and egg. Add flour and salt, mix until shaggy. Knead 10–12 minutes until smooth and slightly tacky — bread flour needs the extra kneading for that chewy texture!
  2. Cover and bulk ferment at room temp until doubled — 5–8 hours. Watch the dough, not the clock.
  3. Punch down and divide into 12 equal pieces (~70g each). Shape into smooth balls, place in a greased 9x13 pan with a little space between each.
  4. Cover loosely and let rise until puffy and touching — 2–4 hours at room temp, or overnight in the fridge (take out 1 hour before baking).
  5. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  6. Brush tops with egg wash. Sprinkle with sesame seeds or flaky salt if using.
  7. Bake 20–23 minutes until deep golden brown. Tent with foil if browning too fast.
  8. Cool in pan 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack. Pull apart and slice horizontally to serve.
Tips
  • 🌊 Water: Start with 200ml, add a tbsp at a time if dough feels too stiff — bread flour absorbs more
  • 🥥 Coconut oil: Melted but not hot — don't cook the egg when mixing!
  • 🥛 Yogurt: Full-fat plain is best. Greek works too — thin slightly with a splash of water
  • ⏰ Make ahead: Second rise in the fridge overnight, bake fresh in the morning
  • ❄️ Freezer: Baked buns freeze great up to 3 months

Sweet Corn Cornbread + Hatch Green Chile Turkey Chili
GF cornbread · Ninja Foodi chili · Perfect together
+
🌽 Cornbread — Ingredients
  • 1 cup canned corn, drained & blended smooth
  • ½ cup whole corn kernels (reserved from the same can)
  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder · ½ tsp baking soda · ½ tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • ¾ cup plain kefir or yogurt
  • ¼ cup coconut oil, melted
  • ¼ cup honey · 1 tsp vanilla extract
🌽 Cornbread — Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease an 8x8 dish with coconut oil.
  2. Drain corn. Set aside ½ cup whole kernels. Blend the rest until completely smooth.
  3. Whisk almond flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk eggs, kefir, coconut oil, honey, vanilla, and corn purée until smooth.
  5. Pour wet into dry, stir until just combined. Fold in reserved corn kernels. Batter will be thick.
  6. Pour into pan and bake until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Cool in pan at least 10 minutes before slicing — key for it to hold together!
🌽 Cornbread — Notes
  • Drizzle honey on top right out of the oven for extra sweetness
  • Reheat in oven (best): foil-wrapped at 325°F for 10 min
  • Reheat in microwave: 20–30 sec in a damp paper towel
  • Reheat in air fryer: 300°F for 3–4 min for a slightly crispy edge
  • Bake in the morning, reheat at dinner — totally solid plan 👍
🫕 Turkey Chili — Ingredients
  • 1 lb frozen ground turkey (block — no thawing needed!)
  • 2 cups homemade beef stock
  • 15 oz canned smashed tomatoes
  • 15 oz black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 4 oz hatch green chiles, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp chili powder · 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp smoked paprika · 1 tsp salt
  • ½ gallon bag frozen kale
🫕 Turkey Chili — Instructions (Ninja Foodi)
  1. Pour beef stock into pot. Place frozen turkey block on top.
  2. Add tomatoes, green chiles, bell pepper, and garlic. Sprinkle spices over the top. Do NOT stir.
  3. Lock lid, set valve to SEAL. Pressure cook on HIGH for 25 minutes.
  4. Quick release. Open lid carefully away from you.
  5. Break up turkey with a spoon, stir everything together. Add black beans and stir to combine.
  6. Stir in frozen kale a few handfuls at a time. Let sit 2–3 minutes until wilted.
  7. Switch to SAUTÉ mode 3–5 minutes uncovered if you want a thicker chili.
🫕 Turkey Chili — Notes
  • Toppings: shredded cheese, sour cream, avocado, or tortilla chips
  • Milder: use half the chiles. Hotter: add a pinch of cayenne
  • Fridge 4 days · Freezer 3 months

"Sea Cracker" Pale Pecan Banana Bread
Maple sweetened · Coconut milk moist · 350°F
+
Ingredients
  • 2 very ripe bananas
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted
  • ¾ cup coconut milk (~half a can)
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup oats
  • 1 tsp baking soda · ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup pecans, roughly chopped
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a loaf pan with butter.
  2. Mash bananas well with a fork until smooth.
  3. Stir in maple syrup, eggs, melted butter, and coconut milk.
  4. Add flour, oats, baking soda, and salt. Stir until just combined — don't overmix!

Notes
  • Stir the coconut milk can well before measuring — cream settles at the top
  • Too thick? Add a splash more coconut milk. Too runny? Don't worry, it bakes out fine!

Peanut Butter Banana Granola
Naturally sweetened · Kid-friendly · 325°F · Extra crunchy clusters
+
Ingredients
  • 5 cups rolled oats
  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 6 ripe bananas, mashed
  • ¾ cup peanut butter
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (163°C). Line two large baking sheets with parchment.
  2. Mash bananas well, then stir in peanut butter, salt, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Add oats, almond flour, and cinnamon. Stir until everything is well coated.
  4. Spread thin across both baking sheets. Bake 20–25 min, stirring halfway. Check at 20 min — almond flour browns faster!

Notes
  • Add after baking: chocolate chips, raisins, coconut flakes, or chopped nuts
  • Stores in an airtight container up to 2 weeks
  • Freezes well too!

Oatmeal Lace Cookies
No milk or butter · Thin & crispy · 325°F
+
Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • ½ cup vegetable oil or coconut oil, melted
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp cinnamon · ½ tsp salt · 1 tsp baking soda
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Line baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Whisk together oil, brown sugar, egg, maple syrup, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Stir in baking soda, cinnamon, and salt. Fold in oats and flour until combined.
  4. Drop small teaspoons of dough at least 3 inches apart — they spread a lot! Flatten as thin as you can with your fingertips.
  5. Bake 8–10 minutes until golden brown all over, not just the edges.
  6. Cool completely on the pan before touching — at least 10 minutes. They firm up and get crispy as they cool!
Notes
